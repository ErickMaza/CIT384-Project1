    My Favorite Things About Uncharted

    These are some of the things that stand out to me the most about the series.

    My Favorite Game

    Uncharted 2: Among Thieves

    Uncharted 2 is my favorite game in the series because of its story, locations, characters, and gameplay.

    It was the first game in the series that I played and i loved the combimation of puzzles and action, and thats why i felt in love with the series.

    My Favorite Character

    Nathan Drake

    Nathan Drake is my favorite character because he is a funny character with his humor. He makes jokes while he is in dangerous situations and he has brown hair like me and for an 8 year old playing the game for the first time he was relatable just because of that.

    My Favorite Part of the Games

    One of my favorite parts of Uncharted is exploring new locations and solving puzzles that lead to more action.

    For me it was really the combination of puzzles and actions that made the series so good.

    Why I Keep Coming Back

    The combination of storytelling and gameplay is what makes the series memorable to me. I've played each game at least 3 times and I still enjoy playing through one of them.
