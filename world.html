    The World of Uncharted

    The Uncharted games take Nathan Drake across the world in search of lost cities, treasures, and historical mysteries.

    Series Main Characters

    Nathan Drake

    The main character of the Uncharted series and a treasure hunter who searches for lost artifacts.

    Victor Sullivan

    Nathan's longtime friend and mentor who joins him on many of his adventures.

    Elena Fisher

    A journalist who becomes an important part of Nathan's life throughout the series.

    Chloe Frazer

    A treasure hunter who works with Nathan during some of his adventures.

    Sam Drake

    Nathan's older brother who plays an important role in Uncharted 4.

    Famous Treasures

    El Dorado

    The legendary treasure Nathan searches for during the first game.

    Cintamani Stone

    A legendary artifact connected to the lost city of Shambhala.

    Ubar

    The lost city known as the Atlantis of the Sands.

    Henry Avery's Treasure

    The massive pirate treasure Nathan searches for in Uncharted 4.
