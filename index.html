    The World of Uncharted

    Uncharted is one of my favorite video game franchises. The series follows main protagonist Nathan Drake as he travels around the world searching for lost treasures and uncovering historical mysteries.

    Explore the Games

    Why Uncharted?

    I like Uncharted because it combines action, exploration, puzzles, and storytelling. Each game takes Nathan Drake to different places and puts him in new situations.

    What This Website Is About

    This website is about the first four main Uncharted games and some of the things that make the series one of my favorite video game franchises.
